> *** Detailed comments: Please justify your recommendation and suggest improvements in technical content or presentation.

1.      Please correct and complete Reference [2], which appears incomplete and may not represent the intended source.
2.      Use the terms accuracy, agreement, correlation, tracking error, and repeatability consistently and according to their statistical meanings.
3.      Describe signal-processing procedures, including:
        o       filtering;
        o       cutoff frequency;
        o       gait-event detection;
        o       time normalization;
        o       resampling;
        o       treatment of missing marker data.
4.      Please complete the information of walking speed, gait-cycle duration, stance duration, and simulator execution duration.
5.      Explain the rationale for using the percentage of points within ±1 SD. This is not a standard measure of agreement and is based on only ten cycles from one participant.
6.      Remove or support the claim that the platform is cost-effective. A bill of materials or approximate total system cost would be useful.
7.      Improve Figure 5 by adding:
        o       simulator variability bands;
        o       peak values;
        o       timing annotations;
        o       an error or residual curve.
8.      Report ground reaction force in both Newtons and percentage of body weight.
9.      Use consistent terminology for the repeatability analysis, for example “intra-device inter-trial repeatability.”
10.     Revise the conclusion to reflect the preliminary nature of the study.
An alternative more appropriate conclusion would be:
“The study demonstrates the preliminary feasibility of a repeatable, position-driven three-DOF gait simulator. Further independent validation of all motion axes, improved kinetic agreement, structural durability testing, and experiments comparing different prosthesis configurations are required before the platform can be considered validated for transtibial prosthesis evaluation.”
